Degree: deg(_)
To get the node's degree count use deg(v).
What it does is it counts the amount of
edges that are connected to the node.

If you try to sum up all of the degrees
of a graph, the amount is always double
the amount of edges in a graph. So
Σ deg(v) where v ∈ V = 2|E|.

A more on the topic can be found in
Characteristics section of this folder.
