--- Task 2: Manual Encryption and Decryption ---
First I checked out the manual and found the list of supported ciphers using "openssh enc -list" command.
Then I started encrypting and decrypting, and for when the key or iv was too short I made it longer.

1.e "openssl enc -chacha20 -e -in plain.txt -out cipher.bin \
       -K 0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef \
       -iv 0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef"
1.d "openssl enc -chacha20 -d -in cipher.bin -out plain.txt \
       -K 0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef \
       -iv 0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef"
in 1.e I encrpyted using chacha20, and in 1.d I decrypted the text encrypted by 1.e. (i.e. chacha20).
To print out the result of a .bin file (from binary to string text) I did "xxd -p cipher.bin".
