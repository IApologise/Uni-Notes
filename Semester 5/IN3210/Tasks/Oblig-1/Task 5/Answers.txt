--- Answer to the task 5a. Words used: 50 ---
SYN Cookies exist to prevent SYN flood attacks - A form of Denial of Service
where the attacker requests a SYN, but never proceeds with an ACK forcing the
server to wait. The idea is to encode the SYN queue entry (our cookie) into
the SYN+ACK, then reconstruct on ACK.

--- Answer to the task 5b. Words used: 20 + 20 + 20 = 60 in total ---
1. Source & Destination IPs. Needed to send to the right server device and
spoof itself as the client device.
2. Source & Destination Ports. Needed to send to the right server process and
spoof itself as the client process.
3. Next TCP sequence & acknowledgement numbers. Needed for validation checks
which the server performs. Acknowledgement may be unnecessary sometimes.
