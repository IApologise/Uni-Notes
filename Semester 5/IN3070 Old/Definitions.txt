atomic: Of simplest degree (like axiom).

propositional: Can either be true (T) or false (F).

formulae: A structured expression.
- {a} is a formula 
- {a ∨ b} is a formula
- ...

eigenvariable: a variable that is bound by a quantifier.
- for example: ∀x or ∃y where x and y are eigenvariables.
- quantifiers: ∀ - true for all, ∃ - true for at least one.

Free variable: A variable that is not in the scope of a quantifier.
Bound variable: A variable that is in the scope of a quantifier.
