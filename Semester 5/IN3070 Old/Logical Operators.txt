Membership: ∈
  Instance on the left is a part of a set on the right.
  In other words, A ∈ B means that A is in a set B.

Universal quantifier: ∀
  Comes with a variable on the right, e.g. ∀x
  The symbol itself means "true for all".

existential quantifier: ∃
  Comes with a variable on the right, e.g. ∃x
  Used to avoid ambiguity.
