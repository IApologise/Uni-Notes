These are the types of reports we contact "Si fra!" (UiO's report
website) about. Note that not all of them must be contacted:
- Daily operation and waste: If something concerns you about
  UiO buildings, waste, or want to report unlawful activity
- HMS deviation: Anything that may pose a threat/rsik.
- Notification from students: Anything you don't like about study
- Notification from employees: Anything illegal that happens at UiO
- PhD candidate: Objectionable circumstances (idk what it is)
- Research ethics: Withdraw from research if it is deemed unethical
