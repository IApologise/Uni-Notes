Risk assessment is the value when we multiply the possibility of
something happening (a risk) with the severity that will. This
is often used to assess whether the resource should be spent
to make it less frequent or less severe when it happens.

Often times you provide reasons for assessing the probability
and severity that may happen. Also, by risk we mean whatever
bad that can happen like getting sick, wounded, damage equipment
and/or buildings, and such.

When assessing the risk we also want to write about how we can
reduce these risks, how much resources it will take to reduce it,
and what risk value we will end up after implementing changes.

There are 3 questions you ask yourself, 1. What can go wrong?
2. What can you do to prevent it, and 3. What can you do to
minimize the damage if it happens?

We assess risk whenever we think there might be any such as
when chemicals are involved, when machines are involved, when
heavy stuff is involved, psychological and social work environment,
overworking causing back pain, and much more.

These parties must be involved in risk assessment:
- Work environment representative
- Employer & Employees
