SiO Helse is of course for students and is made
to make sure that they are in good condition.

Studentombud is where students can get some
help or clarification to ensure their welfare.



It is employers that must inform and dictate to
the employees their HMS work and role.

Whatever it is that may harm the environment
must be informed to the employers / leaders.

Safety representatives must make sure that
the interests of employees concerning working
environment are dealt with as well as the
Working Environment Act is compiled with.

The manager is responsible for safety
representatives and their work.

Working environment committees are the parties
that must also monitor the issues concerning
safety, health, and welfare of employees.



IDF (Informasjon- og Drøftingsmøter):
IDF is a regular and repetitive meeting
between employers with representatives of
the civil seervant organizations as observers.
They are there to oversee the process.

BHT (Bedriftshelsetjenesten):
BHT is made of UiOs leaders and employees which
are focused on creating opportunities for work.

EIR (Enhet for Intern Revisjon):
EIR assists UiOs management to ensure that
internal controls have been established properly.

Personverneombud:
UiO also takes care of personal interests, that of
employers, employees, and of course the students.
