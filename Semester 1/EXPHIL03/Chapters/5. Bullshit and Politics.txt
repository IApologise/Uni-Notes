About Socrates:
- Socrates is tied to utilitarian ethics (dydsetikk).
- He never wrote, but is mentioned by other in scripts.
- He actively tried to reason and answer philosophical questions (gods, ...).
- He was unpopular because he questioned authority.
- Eventually executed for many reasons (exactly: powerpoint presentation).
- Argues that if you know something, you can defend this knowledge well.
