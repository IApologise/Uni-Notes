Helper Instructions:
| FROM {Table}  ->  Selects data from specific table
| WHERE {Collumn} = {Data}  ->  Selects data of specific row
| CASCADE  ->  Deletes everything related to whatever is being deleted



Instruction Examples:
|
| DELETE
|   FROM Students
|  WHERE birthdate > '1990-01-01';
