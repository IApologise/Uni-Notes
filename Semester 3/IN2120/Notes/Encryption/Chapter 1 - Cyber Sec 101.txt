These are the most important goals to keep track of.
All of those goals are to be integrated into the system:
1. Confidentiality
2. Integrity
3. Accessability
4. Traceability
5. Reliability



Confidentiality:
It is about keeping user data confidential, so that only
those that should access the data, can access the data.

Integrity:
This is about making sure that data can not be modified
or altered in any way by unauthorized users of processes.

Accessability:
The data that should be accessed must be available
at all times as the user wishes to.

Traceability:
In case of breach of integrity the culprits / actors
are to be traced down by the logs, or some other methods.

Reliability:
In case of breach or poor implementation of some parts
the overall system must not budge and stay vigilant.
Meaning, if part A fails, part B of the system should
not be affected and work as usual without problems.
