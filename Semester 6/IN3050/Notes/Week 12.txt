Policy:
How an AI chooses to act based on observations. So a transition function,
a map of sorts from inputs (observations) to outputs (actions). It may
be determenistic or stochastic (non-deterministic, random). The reason why
we may want for it to be stochastic because of exploration vs exploitation.

Supervisor:
This is a part of environemnt that rewards, punishes, or ignores the AI
based on how well its actions performed in the environment. 

Agent:
This is what updates the Policy weights during training. Once the feedback
from the supervisor is received the agent learns from the feedback and either
reinforces similar action it made in the environment or the opposite.
