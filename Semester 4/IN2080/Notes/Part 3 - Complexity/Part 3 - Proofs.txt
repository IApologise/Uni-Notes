--- Intro ---
There are 3 types of facts, the difference between them is moslty the magnitude and depth of its use in proofs:
- Theorem: A major result that is considered important in its own right.
- Lemma: A supporting result used primarily to prove a theorem or another result.
- Corollary: A result that follows relatively directly from a theorem.
P.S. The examples below assume that you know what SAT and 3SAT are.
